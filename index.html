About

I'm about to start my freshman year in Electrical Engineering at San José State University, with an eye toward a minor in Artificial Intelligence. I'm especially drawn to power electronics and power systems — the technology behind how energy is generated, converted, and delivered at scale.

Before college, I spent a lot of time doing scientific research — three papers across cancer biology, particle size analysis, and anesthesiology — which sharpened how I think through a problem methodically, from question to evidence to conclusion. I'm now applying that same rigor to engineering, currently working through Harvard's CS50 course on Artificial Intelligence with Python.

Outside the classroom, I've mentored kids in robotics and computer science, earned my Eagle Scout rank leading a team of 20+ scouts through a hands-on conservation project, hold BLS (Basic Life Support) certification, and serve in student leadership roles. Being minutes from Silicon Valley, I'm always looking for ways to get closer to the industry while I build my foundation.
